# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (2026-11-17)

First public release.

- **Update watch:** finds ToS and Privacy Policy update emails, reads the new documents on the official site, compares them with the previous version (company changelog, Open Terms Archive, ToS;DR, or the Wayback Machine), and flags concerns against a 15-category red-flag list.
- **New sign-up snapshot:** reviews what you agreed to when signing up, surfaces deadlines that start at sign-up (arbitration opt-outs, trial ends, refund windows), checks what you connected, and records a baseline.
- **Safety:** phishing check, prompt-injection guard, ask-before-acting, and guidance on when to bring in a lawyer.
- **Reports:** deadlines first, 🔴/🟡/🟢 ratings, "what this means for you," and whether each clause is normal or unusual.
